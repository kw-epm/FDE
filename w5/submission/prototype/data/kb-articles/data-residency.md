# Data residency and compliance

CloudServe operates two data regions: **us-east-1** (default) and **eu-west-1** (Enterprise opt-in).

## Compliance posture

- SOC 2 Type II (annual, public report on request).
- ISO 27001.
- HIPAA-eligible mode for Enterprise (BAA required).
- GDPR-ready: DPA on file, EU-region option.

## When to escalate

Any customer asking about a regulatory inquiry, audit, or DPA terms → **escalate to Uma Bardwaj (Compliance)**. Do not let the AI improvise on contractual or compliance questions.
