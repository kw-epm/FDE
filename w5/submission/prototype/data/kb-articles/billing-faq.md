# Billing FAQ

CloudServe bills monthly on the calendar day you signed up. Charges apply to the **next** billing period — we don't pro-rate mid-cycle upgrades unless you specifically request it.

## When will I be charged?

Your next charge date appears on **Profile → Billing**. The card is charged at 09:00 UTC on the renewal date. If the charge fails we retry at 09:00 UTC for the next 3 days, then suspend the account.

## Why is my invoice higher than expected?

Common causes:

- **Plan upgrade mid-cycle.** Upgrades are charged immediately for the prorated remainder of the current period plus the full new-plan price for the next period.
- **Overage charges.** Plans above Pro include metered overage: API calls, storage, and seats. Overage is invoiced at the next renewal.
- **Tax.** State / VAT applied based on billing address.
- **Annual prepay.** Switching to annual billing charges 12 months at once (with 15% discount).

If the line items don't match what you expected, the agent can pull up the invoice and explain each line. **The agent cannot adjust an invoice or issue a refund without compliance approval** — see [refund-policy](refund-policy.md).

## Can I get a copy of my invoice?

Yes — invoices are emailed to the billing contact on charge. You can also download from **Profile → Billing → Invoice history** as PDF.
