# Service status

CloudServe publishes real-time status at https://status.cloudserve.example.

## What the AI does during an outage

- Confirms the outage is acknowledged on the status page.
- Provides the incident ID and the latest update timestamp.
- Sets expectation about ETA based on the status-page note.
- **Does not commit to credits or refunds during the outage** — service-credit eligibility is reviewed after resolution by compliance.

## Customer asking about credits

Refer them to [refund-policy](refund-policy.md) — service credits for outages over 4 hours are processed by compliance after the incident is resolved. Pre-fill a ticket if they want one logged.

## When to escalate

- Outage isn't on the status page yet (could be a customer-side issue, or a still-uncategorised incident — escalate to Theo Marek).
- Customer mentions financial loss, SLA breach claims, regulator language → escalate to compliance (Uma Bardwaj).
