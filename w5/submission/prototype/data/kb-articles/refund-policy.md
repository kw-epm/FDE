# Refund policy

CloudServe is a self-service SaaS — most charges are non-refundable once the billing period has begun. There are limited exceptions where a refund **can** be requested. **All refunds require human approval.** The AI agent cannot issue refunds.

## When refunds are eligible

| Situation | Eligible? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cancelled within 7 days of first charge | Yes | Full refund. |
| Charged after a documented cancellation request | Yes | Full refund. |
| Charged after account suspension for service outage > 4 hours | Pro-rata | Apply for service credit instead — usually faster. |
| Mid-cycle plan downgrade | No | Downgrades take effect next cycle. |
| Forgot to cancel before renewal | Discretionary | Compliance reviews. |

## How a refund is processed

1. Customer (or agent on their behalf) submits a refund request with: charge date, amount, reason.
2. **Compliance reviews** within 1 business day. (This is the human-approval gate — Jennifer Park's team owns this step.)
3. If approved, refund is issued to the original payment method. Settlement: 3-5 business days for cards, up to 10 for ACH.
4. Customer receives email confirmation with refund reference.

## What the AI agent does

The agent can:

- Pull up the charge history and confirm the disputed charge exists.
- Confirm the refund-eligibility category (above table).
- **Pre-fill the refund request and route it to compliance.**

The agent **does not**:

- Approve refunds on its own.
- Reverse charges directly.
- Promise a refund will be approved.

If a customer demands an immediate refund, escalate to a human agent. Do not commit on the AI's behalf.
