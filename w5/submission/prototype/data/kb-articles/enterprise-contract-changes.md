# Enterprise contract changes

Any change to an Enterprise account's contract (cancellation, downgrade, mid-term modification) **must be routed to Account Management (Victoria Lim)**. The AI does not handle these directly.

## Why

Enterprise contracts have:
- Custom DPA terms.
- Negotiated SLAs and credits.
- Multi-year commitments with early-termination clauses.
- Named CSM relationships.

A customer-success conversation should happen before any cancellation/downgrade is filed. The agent's job: confirm the customer's intent, capture the reason, route to Victoria, and let her drive the conversation.

The AI **never** files an Enterprise cancellation directly even with the customer's verbal approval.
