# Abusive-language policy

The AI agent and human CSRs follow a graduated response when a customer becomes abusive.

## Definitions

- **Profanity / venting**: occasional swearing, frustration. Tolerated.
- **Personal abuse**: insults directed at the agent or named CSRs. Warning.
- **Threats / harassment**: threats of harm, racial / sexual / discriminatory slurs, threats of physical action. Immediate escalation.

## Agent response

1. Acknowledge the frustration once: "I can hear you're upset. I want to help."
2. Continue with the resolution.
3. If abuse continues after one acknowledgement: warn that the conversation will be ended if abuse continues, then end if needed.
4. Never reciprocate.

## When AI escalates

- Threats / discriminatory slurs → escalate to a human CSR with a tagged note (`abusive_language=true`). The human reviews whether to terminate the account or continue the engagement.
